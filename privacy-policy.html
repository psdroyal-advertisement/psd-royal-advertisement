Privacy Policy

Effective date: 21 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how PSD Royal Advertisement may collect, use and protect information when people contact us or request our services.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on your interaction with us, we may collect your name, email address, phone number, business details, advertising requirements, project photographs, logos, communications, feedback and transaction-related records.

2. How We Use Information

We use information to respond to enquiries, prepare quotations, understand your business, plan and deliver advertising projects, communicate with customers, maintain business records and meet legal obligations.

3. Payments

When a third-party payment provider processes a transaction, that provider may collect and handle payment information under its own terms and privacy policy. Do not send passwords, PINs or payment-card security codes to us by email or through ordinary website enquiries.

4. Sharing Information

We do not sell customers' personal information. We may share relevant information with service providers who help us operate our business and deliver requested services, or where disclosure is required by law or necessary to protect legal rights.

5. Project Materials and AI Tools

Materials supplied for a project may be used to plan, create and deliver the requested advertising work. Some projects may involve AI-assisted tools or external production platforms. The handling of information by those providers may also be subject to their own terms and privacy practices.

6. Data Security and Retention

We take reasonable steps to protect information from unauthorised access, loss, misuse or disclosure. We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary for project delivery, recordkeeping, dispute resolution and legal obligations. No electronic system can be guaranteed completely secure.

7. Cookies and Website Services

Our website hosting provider or embedded services may use technical features necessary to operate the website. If we introduce analytics or advertising tracking technologies, we will provide appropriate information and choices where required by law.

8. Your Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access to, correction of or deletion of personal information we hold about you. Contact us by email to make a request. We may need to verify your identity and retain certain records where legally required.

9. Third-Party Websites

Our website may link to external websites or services. Those providers have their own privacy practices, and we encourage you to review their policies before submitting information to them.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as our business or legal requirements change. The latest version and its effective date will be published on this page.

11. Contact Us
